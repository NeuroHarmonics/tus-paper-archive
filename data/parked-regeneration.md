# Parked papers

Papers excluded by a **scope ruling** rather than by a fact about the paper. Each is held here so the ruling can be reversed without a new sweep.

## neural regeneration  (18 papers)

**Excluded under E9**: the endpoint is tissue repaired or grown, not neural activity changed. The reasoning: if regeneration is not a mechanism for primary ultrasound neuromodulation, it does not belong, and at roughly 158 sweep candidates it is large enough to reshape the archive if admitted.
