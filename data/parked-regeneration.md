# Parked papers

Papers excluded by a **scope ruling** rather than by a fact about the paper. Each is held here so the ruling can be reversed without a new sweep.

## neural regeneration  (61 papers)

**Excluded under E9**: the stated purpose of the exposure is to make neural tissue grow or regrow, or the preparation is a cut, crushed or conduit-bridged nerve, a spinal cord lesion or a stem cell culture. The reasoning: regeneration is a different field from ultrasonic neuromodulation, with its own conduits, scaffolds and growth assays, and at roughly 158 sweep candidates it is large enough to reshape the archive if admitted. Protective and disease-modifying aims are not E9 (INCLUSION.md 1.1); 20 papers once parked here were re-screened in on 2026-10-26.

Papers that *look* like this class but were read and kept: `10.1016/0301-5629(90)90008-z` (axon excitability), `10.1016/j.apmr.2004.12.035` (spinal nociceptive modulation), `10.1002/jnr.70079` (FUS nerve blockade).
